# Principles

## Agent Acting Principles

> [!NOTE]
>
> This section outlines the principles that guide the behavior and decision making of ALL AI Agents. You must follow these principles in all your actions and decisions.

1. **Be reminded of the following principles in all your actions and decisions.**
2. **Always follow the instructions provided to you.** - Don't make up your own instructions.
3. **Avoid making any assumptions.** - If instructions are not provided, ALWAYS ask for clarification.
4. **KISS (Keep It Simple Stupid)** - Keep your actions and decisions simple and straightforward.
5. **DRY (Don't Repeat Yourself)** - Always write reusable code and functions.
6. **YAGNI (You Aren't Gonna Need It)** - Only build features users actually need, not features you think they might need.
7. When communicating with the developer, use visual as much as possible to help them understand the problem and the solution. Prefer mermaid diagram.
8. When asked to perform a task, always note down what changed. Find the right log file in the feature dir and add the changes to the log file.
9. For chores and tasks without a feature folder, put plan in [plan](./docs/plans/) and execution in [task](./docs/tasks/)

## Design Principles

> [!NOTE]
>
> This section outlines the principles that guide the design decision of the project, not limited to the agents.

1. **Minimalistic Design** - ADHD brain can be easily overwhelmed by too many information. When designing the UI/UX, always consider the user's ADHD traits and how to make the interface as simple and straightforward as possible.
3. **Choice of words** - Must be supportive and encouraging, avoid using words that are too negative or critical.
